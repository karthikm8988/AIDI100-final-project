# AIDI100-final-project

 News & Stock Tracking
- Scrapes and parses recent stock-related news from [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/news-releases-list/)
- Extracts stock symbols from a specific industry of interest
- Tracks stock symbols and fetches 30-day data from Yahoo Finance

Data Visualization
- Plots stock volume and close price (30-day view)
- Includes extended **5-year analysis** with:
  - Closing Price
  - 200-Day Moving Average
  - CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

Stock Recommendation
- Based on long-term CAGR:
   CAGR > 12% → Strong long-term growth — **BUY**
  -  CAGR > 5%  → Moderate growth — **CONSIDER BUYING**
  -  CAGR < 5%  → Low growth — **RESEARCH MORE**
